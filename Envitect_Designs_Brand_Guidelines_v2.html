Usage Rules

Barlow Condensed for all headlines, navigation, buttons, and UI text. Use 700 for impact, 400 for body.

Cormorant Garamond for taglines, pull quotes, and editorial moments only. Never for UI or forms.

DM Mono for labels, codes, metadata, technical specs, and section numbers.

Minimum body text size: 14px digital / 9pt print. Line height: 1.5× for body, 1.0× for display.

Design Principles
01
Precision First

Every element earns its place. We design with technical accuracy—clean lines, deliberate spacing, and structured composition that reflects our engineering roots.

02
Environment Conscious

The teal arc in our mark is a promise. Sustainable thinking is woven into every visual choice—warmth, organic curves, earthy paper tones against structural ink.

03
Confident Clarity

We don't decorate. We communicate. Visual hierarchy is strict, whitespace is generous, and every design reduces complexity rather than adding to it.
